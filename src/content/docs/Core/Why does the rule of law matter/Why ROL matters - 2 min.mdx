When people trust the rules, they can plan, invest, speak freely, and participate in public life. Together, they can create opportunity, stability, and peace.

<Aside type="tip" title="The bottom line">
The rule of law is not an abstract idea. It is the foundation of dignity, security, prosperity, and freedom. It is what makes freedom real. It is what makes justice possible. And it is what makes power accountable.
</Aside>
