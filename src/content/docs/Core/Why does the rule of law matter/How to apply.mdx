## The rule of law is foundational to people’s everyday lives. 

Develop tailored narratives for:
* Individuals: Access to justice, safety, dignity 
* Businesses: Predictability, investment, risk reduction 
* Governments / reformers: Legitimacy, effectiveness 

## Key approach

<Aside type="note" title="Use real-life stories:">
* Explain mechanisms clearly (e.g., why weak courts affect jobs or prices) 
* Show how problems people face are rule-of-law problems 
* Consult stories in [People Narratives](core/why-does-the-rule-of-law-matter/people-narratives/)
</Aside>
 

<Aside type="note" title="Include an aspirational dimension:">
* With rule of law → opportunity, trust, growth 
* Without it → barriers, uncertainty, abuse 
</Aside>
